Privacy Policy — Brawl Helper

Status: preliminary development version. Before store publication, this policy will be completed with the data controller identity, the providers actually used, retention periods, and the details of the advertising and analytics services actually integrated.

1. What is Brawl Helper?

Brawl Helper is an unofficial guide and companion application for Brawl Stars. It is not sponsored, endorsed, or operated by Supercell.

2. Game profile data

You may enter your Player Tag. The tag is used to request publicly available game data through the Brawl Helper backend and authorized game APIs. The Player Tag is stored locally on the device/browser so the selected profile can be restored.

3. Data we do not request

Brawl Helper does not request Supercell passwords, authentication credentials, authentication cookies, contacts, microphone, camera, or precise location for its core functionality.

4. Backend and security

Backend requests are transmitted over HTTPS. Credentials used to access game APIs are kept out of the client and must never be published in the repository or application.

5. Caching

A profile may be temporarily stored in local cache and, for technical purposes, in the backend. The cache is associated with the requested Player Tag and is not an authentication system.

6. Analytics and advertising

Any analytics and advertising services will be explicitly identified before they are introduced into production. The final policy will specify what data is collected, the purposes, which providers receive the data, and how consent is managed.

7. Aggregate data

Any community statistics, such as build-usage trends, will be designed in aggregated form and, where required, on the basis of user consent. Brawl Helper will not publish an individual user's private profile as community data.

8. Retention and deletion

The final policy will specify retention periods for each data category and the available deletion procedures. If a real Brawl Helper account is introduced in the future, an account and associated-data deletion process will be provided in accordance with store requirements.

9. Rights and contact

Before publication, the data controller, privacy contact, and instructions for exercising applicable data-protection rights will be added.

10. Supercell content

This material is unofficial and is not endorsed by Supercell. For more information, see the Supercell Fan Content Policy.
